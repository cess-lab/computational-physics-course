From a rate to a step

The initial condition is 80 °C at t = 0 s. Euler’s method calculates the rate at the current temperature, holds it constant over one timestep and uses it to estimate the next value.

The markers are computed values; the straight lines join them for reading.

Check against a reference

The supplied exact solution is T(t) = 20 + 60 exp(−t/τ) °C. Compare Euler and exact temperatures at the same time, then reduce Δt and compare the error at 300 s.
