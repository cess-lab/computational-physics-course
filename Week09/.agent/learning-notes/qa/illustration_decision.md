# Embedded illustration decision

No ImageGen illustration is used for Week 09. The central representation barrier is the relationship among measured points, the fitted line, and signed residuals. Native MATLAB graphs carry the exact axes, units, marker positions, fitted values, and residual magnitudes needed for that reasoning. A generated resistor illustration would be decorative and would not clarify the fitting operation as directly as the data and residual plots.

The retained MATLAB figures are strict numerical assets generated by `../../matlab/generate_week09_reference_assets.m`. They are embedded directly in the learning note with deterministic captions and are separately retained for future slide-reference QA.
