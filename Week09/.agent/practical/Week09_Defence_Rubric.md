# Week 09 Individual Defence Rubric

Score the selected student out of 8. Record every attempt as a new row in `Week09_Defence_Rotation_Ledger.csv`; use `Week09_Defence_Member_Summary.csv` only to identify who remains eligible for a first selection. Record one completed defence for every group member before selecting any member a second time. An absence remains pending until its recorded make-up defence is completed.

| Criterion | 0 marks | 1 mark | 2 marks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physical model, variables, and units | Cannot connect the fitted relationship to the context. | Names some variables or units. | Explains measured quantities, fitted parameter, equation, and parameter unit. |
| Fit and residual trace | Cannot identify the fitting or residual step. | Identifies one command or residual sign. | Traces the supplied fit/prediction/residual logic and explains a residual sign in context. |
| Validation and assigned variant | No usable check. | States a check without applying it. | Applies a reference, residual-pattern, repeatability, or unit check to answer the assigned variant. |
| Interpretation and AI judgement | No physical conclusion. | Gives an unsupported conclusion. | Interprets the fitted result, states a limitation or uncertainty boundary, and explains the recorded independent AI checks. |

The 3% cumulative individual-defence component averages each student's completed recorded entries. A polished fit or high fit-quality statistic does not replace the student's explanation of the model, units, residual evidence, validation, and physical result.
