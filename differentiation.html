From slope to velocity

Velocity is the rate of change of position with time. The secant gives an average velocity between two sampled positions; it estimates the instantaneous velocity at t₀.

Its units are metres divided by seconds, or m/s.

Check the estimate

The analytic derivative is v(t) = 20 − 9.81t m/s. Compare it with the numerical estimate at the same t₀ and report the absolute error.

For this quadratic model, central difference agrees with the analytic derivative to floating-point precision. This special result does not hold for every function.
