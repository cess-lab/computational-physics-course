# Week 06 Variant Bank

Use only after group submissions are locked. Safe variants include changing the target time, changing one supplied step size, changing the initial speed, changing the potential length scale, changing the cooling constant, inserting a denominator defect that uses `t0` instead of `h`, swapping the order of two sampled values to reverse the sign, or asking the student to identify whether a stated derivative unit is physically consistent.

Keep the defence focused on physical rate meaning, the two values used by the finite difference, step-size interpretation, units, analytic/reference validation, simple error, and physical meaning. Do not require error-order derivations, detailed truncation-versus-round-off analysis, or higher-order formulas as Core defence evidence.
