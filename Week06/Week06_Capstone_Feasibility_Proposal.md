# Week 06 Capstone Feasibility Proposal

This is a capstone feasibility checkpoint, not a separate course-mark component. Submit one concise proposal per group through Google Classroom. The final capstone is judged through one rubric whose criteria total 100%; the lecturer explains the overall course contribution separately.

Your task is to show that your assigned bounded brief can become a manageable computational investigation. You are **not** required to lock the final numerical method this week. The approved model, method, and validation plan are the Week 07 checkpoint.

## Feasibility test

Before submission, your group should be able to answer **yes** to all five questions:

- Is the physical question narrow enough to answer with one principal output?
- Are the required inputs or data available or realistically obtainable?
- Can one parameter or assumption be modified without changing everything at once?
- Is there at least one independent validation anchor?
- Can every group member explain the model, variables, units, and intended output?

## AI-use record

AI is allowed for the capstone. Record only material decisions:

- what independent check you used before accepting any important claim.

Do not submit a complete AI chat transcript.

## Submission boundary

Keep this milestone concise. Do not write a full report, implement a complete project from scratch, or force a numerical method choice before Week 07. Google Classroom is the official submission record.
