PHY4605 WEEK 05 IMAGEGEN FIXED MASTER CONTRACT

Purpose
This contract is mandatory for every remaining Week 05 raster slide. The slide-specific handoff supplies content; this file locks the visual execution and acceptance criteria.

Canonical references
1. Week02/.agent/lecture-slides/raster/slide-02.png for workflow/card rhythm and title/subtitle hierarchy.
2. Week03/.agent/lecture-slides/raster/slide-01.png for scientific linework, equation/diagram balance, and restrained colour semantics.
3. Week05/.agent/lecture-slides/raster/slide-05.png for the approved Week 05 title band, safe margins, card stroke, and takeaway treatment.

Fixed canvas
- Exactly 1920 x 1080 px, 16:9.
- Pure flat white #FFFFFF background.
- No border, frame, background wash, vignette, gradient, texture, node motif, shadow field, logo, watermark, URL, citation, source label, footer, or page number.
- Every visible element must remain at least 76 px from all four edges. Prefer 115 px left/right.

Fixed title band
- Title anchor: x=115 px, y=60 px.
- Title: Roboto Condensed Bold or one visually identical condensed sans-serif; sentence case; navy #0B2B4C; nominal 68 px; maximum two lines; no terminal full stop.
- Subtitle: x=115 px; directly below title; Roboto Medium or a non-condensed supporting sans-serif; 30 px; charcoal #3F4B57; no bold condensed face; no terminal full stop.
- Do not enlarge the title to fill unused space. Match the visual scale of approved Week05 slide-05.png.

Fixed content language
- Use the exact slide-specific wording from Week05_Lecture_Slide_Deck_Handoff.md. Do not paraphrase, add prefixes, add labels, add punctuation, or create explanatory copy.
- Preserve mathematical minus signs, primes, subscripts, superscripts, inequality signs, units, brackets, decimal places, and scientific notation exactly.
- Use navy for structure/equations, blue for model/residual, green/teal for validity, gold for numerical choice/iteration, red only for failure/warning, and purple only for fzero/reference-solver content.
- Cards use white or the specified very pale semantic fill, 2 px fine stroke, 20-24 px rounded corners, and no visible shadow.
- Bottom takeaway/warning strips use the exact fill/stroke/text colour specified in the handoff. Never substitute a dark banner unless the handoff explicitly requests it.
- Use functional scientific diagrams only. No decorative imagery.

Generation rule
- Generate one complete slide as one ImageGen raster. Do not use SVG, HTML, Canvas, Graphviz, PowerPoint shapes, text overlays, or post-generation visible edits.
- Do not submit an image that was merely padded if the title family, subtitle family, punctuation, exact copy, or scientific diagram is wrong. Regenerate or edit through ImageGen.
- Proportional white padding is allowed only to correct edge clearance when every content and typography requirement already passes.

Binary pre-submission checklist
PASS only when every answer is YES:
1. Is the file exactly 1920 x 1080?
2. Is the canvas pure flat white and borderless?
3. Are all visible elements >=76 px from every edge?
4. Does the title match the approved Week 05 scale, face, colour, and x/y anchor?
5. Is the subtitle non-condensed charcoal supporting text with no terminal full stop?
6. Is every visible word and punctuation mark identical to the handoff?
7. Are every equation, number, unit, sign, subscript, and superscript exact?
8. Does the diagram/table/chart support the intended scientific claim without ambiguity?
9. Do accent colours follow the semantic mapping?
10. Is there no branding, footer, source label, URL, background decoration, or unrequested element?

Required return
- Absolute raster path.
- Exact statement: Fixed master checklist 10/10 PASS.
- One-line unresolved risk, or “Unresolved risk: none.”
- Stop and wait for coordinator audit. Do not begin another slide.
