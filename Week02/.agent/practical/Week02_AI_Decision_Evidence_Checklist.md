# Week 02 AI Decision-Evidence Checklist

Use this concise record in the group's completed evidence. Do not request a complete AI chat history.

## Required group record

- [ ] AI tool and version/model named, or `AI not used` stated clearly.
- [ ] Assistance requested described in one or two sentences (formulation, code, debugging, explanation, or refinement).
- [ ] Material decision recorded: what was accepted, modified, or rejected.
- [ ] Reason for that decision tied to the supplied model, units, code trace, output, or a known result.
- [ ] Independent checks named and shown: at least one unit/scale or model check, one known/limiting/reference result where applicable, and a physical interpretation check.
- [ ] Final work was rerun from a fresh MATLAB session and the group can identify each member's contribution.

## Instructor completeness test

Accept the record when it shows judgement, not merely tool disclosure. A runnable AI-generated loop without a trace and validation is insufficient; a precise diagnosis of a failed run can still earn the relevant evidence. Do not use AI-detection scores as a misconduct measure.
