# Week 02 Group Evidence Rubric

Score the completed ten-question group packet on a 0–3 band for each criterion. Convert each band to the stated weight with `weighted score = (band / 3) × criterion weight`. The packet score is out of 100; the course portfolio still requires all ten weekly submissions and counts the best eight for the group component.

| Criterion | Weight | 3 — secure | 2 — developing | 1 — limited | 0 — not evidenced |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model, variables, and units | 15% | Correctly identifies supplied assumptions, variables, units, and expected trends in all three contexts. | Main models and units are correct with minor omissions. | Names some quantities but confuses a unit, parameter, or physical meaning. | No usable model/units evidence. |
| Algorithm and pseudocode | 20% | Ordered plan clearly links inputs, current value, update, storage, output, and check; loop stopping is clear. | Plan is mostly ordered but needs a small prompt or omits one step. | Fragments are present but dependencies or loop logic are unclear. | No meaningful plan. |
| Code reading, completion, and modification | 25% | Trace is accurate; supplied loops are completed or repaired; one parameter change is controlled and explained. | Code/output link is mostly correct with a minor error or prompt. | Some code runs or is copied, but the index, update, or modification is not explained. | No traceable implementation or precise execution diagnosis. |
| Validation | 15% | Uses appropriate initial-value, half-life, trend, or quarter-period checks and states what each check tests. | Performs a valid check with limited explanation. | Names a check but does not connect it to the result. | No valid check. |
| Physical interpretation and limitation | 20% | Explains graphs, sign/trend, parameter effect, and one limitation of the supplied idealised models. | Gives a plausible interpretation with a minor omission. | Describes output without a clear physical conclusion or limitation. | No physical interpretation. |
| Responsible AI decision record | 5% | Clearly records tool/use, accepted/modified/rejected assistance, reason, and independent checks; or explicitly records no AI use. | Record is present but one decision or check is thin. | Disclosure is vague or checks are not identified. | No record. |

## Application notes

- Do not grade code length, MATLAB function-name memorisation, or polished formatting.
- Full numerical-output credit requires reproducible output or a precise diagnosis of why execution failed. A correct model or trace can still earn its criterion when execution is incomplete.
- The Week02 challenge's three contexts are supplied analogous models; do not add unfamiliar physics as a hidden requirement.
- Stretch evidence—an independently written reusable function, a second independent check, or a method comparison—may distinguish excellent work but is not required for an ordinary pass.
