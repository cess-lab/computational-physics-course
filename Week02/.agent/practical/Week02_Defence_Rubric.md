# Week 02 Individual Defence Rubric

Score the selected student's defence from 0 to 3 in each dimension. The four-category mean is the student's Week02 entry in the cumulative 3% individual-defence ledger.

| Dimension | 3 — secure | 2 — developing | 1 — limited | 0 — not evidenced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model and units | Explains the supplied model, variables, units, assumptions, and expected trend. | Explains the main model with one minor omission. | Recites some values without a coherent model/unit link. | Cannot explain the relevant model. |
| Code and output trace | Traces the relevant loop pass, index, update, and output accurately. | Traces most of the line/output link with a small prompt. | Recognises fragments but cannot connect code to output reliably. | Cannot trace the relevant code/output. |
| Validation or variant reasoning | Applies the assigned check, parameter change, or defect repair and explains why it is informative. | Responds correctly with limited explanation or prompting. | States a check/variant result without linking it to the model. | No valid check or variant response. |
| Physical interpretation and AI judgement | Gives a defensible physical conclusion and explains how AI output was accepted, changed, rejected, or independently checked; “AI not used” is acceptable when stated clearly. | Gives a plausible conclusion and partial AI-use judgement. | Gives a vague conclusion or disclosure. | No physical interpretation or judgement. |

Apply the no-repeat rule before scoring: establish one completed defence entry for every group member before any member receives a second selection. An absence is pending, not a zero; score it only after the recorded make-up defence.
