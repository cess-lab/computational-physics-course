## Sequence

- Prediction poll before revealing the vertical-motion explanation.
- Loop trace before the worked index trace: index 2 selects 0.5 s and stores y_m(2); reference height 8.77375 m.
- Storage completion: current_position_m belongs in y_m(sample_index). A fixed index overwrites the first slot.
- Parameter prediction before the comparison graph: lower initial velocity gives a lower peak.
- Pair pseudocode on the 12-group wall, followed by a concise QR response. The supplied decay model has checks N(0)=800, N(6 h)=400, N(24 h)=50 counts.
- Debug clinic before the worked defect slide. Group A: missing end; B: fixed storage index; C: wrong gravity sign. Students state evidence, repair and validation. Zoom into one case to read the native code.
- Validation poll: y(0)=0 cannot detect the wrong gravity sign. Compare a nonzero known value and the physical shape.
- Individual exit ticket and confidence scale 1-5. Read a small sample and address one common misconception.

## Direct canvas collaboration

Assign pairs or trios to group spaces; nominal 35 students fit 12 trios/pairs. Students type in or duplicate a sticky, comment with an evidence question on another group, and revise after discussion. Reactions: thumbs up = can explain, thinking = need an example, question = pause. Preserve supplied equations/code; use response stickies for repairs.

Keep formal submissions, feedback and due dates in Google Classroom. These are ungraded live learning activities, not the pre-practical Form quiz. After class, the lecturer can choose when to return public-link access to Viewer.
