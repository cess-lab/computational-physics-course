# Week 08 Individual Defence Rubric

Score the selected student out of 8. Record every attempt as a new row in `Week08_Defence_Rotation_Ledger.csv`; use `Week08_Defence_Member_Summary.csv` only to identify who remains eligible for a first selection. Record one completed defence for every group member before selecting any member a second time. An absence remains pending until a recorded make-up defence is completed.

| Criterion | 0 marks | 1 mark | 2 marks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model, variables, and units | Cannot connect equation to the context. | Names some variables or units. | Explains the supplied model, sign convention where relevant, initial condition, and units. |
| Euler code trace | Cannot explain the update. | Identifies the loop or one variable. | Traces one update and connects the slope, timestep, and index to the physics. |
| Validation and variant | No usable check. | States a check without applying it. | Uses the supplied reference, half-life, bound, or timestep evidence to answer the assigned variant. |
| Interpretation and AI judgement | No physical conclusion. | Gives an unsupported conclusion. | Interprets the output, states one limitation, and explains the recorded independent AI checks. |

The 3% cumulative individual-defence component averages each student's completed recorded entries. A polished script does not replace the student's explanation of model, code/output, validation, and physical result.
