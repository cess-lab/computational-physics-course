# Week 12 Studio Defence Guide

Week 12 is a formative capstone checkpoint. Do not add a new portfolio packet, change course weights, or count rehearsal scores in the cumulative 3% practical-defence component. Use the existing approved capstone model, starter and validation plan; students are not asked to invent a new investigation.

## Completion Evidence

Confirm that the group has a bounded question, supplied/approved model with assumptions and units, a short pseudocode plan, one justified parameter or assumption modification, one principal labelled output, the required check plus one chosen check, physical interpretation, one limitation, a fresh-session reproducibility record, and concise AI decisions. A chosen check must address a real claim and explain its limit; a smooth graph is not enough.

The group should rehearse with every member. For challenge-slot rehearsal, lock the evidence before drawing the slot/variant and individual member. Use distinct variants where slots repeat. Import the existing group rotation history before a random draw; never restart eligibility at Week 12. No student is reselected within a group until every member has been selected. Record absence as pending and arrange a make-up. Rehearsal itself does not create a graded practical-defence entry.

For the separate graded cumulative practical ledger, retain the course rule: each student completes a first defence before any receives a second; thereafter average completed entries, keeping absence pending until make-up completion. This studio guide does not alter that ledger.

## Feedback Rubric

Use the four rows as feedback prompts; optional local 0/1/2 indicators are readiness signals, not marks.

| Evidence | Needs support | Developing | Ready to explain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model and method | Cannot link output to model | Names method with incomplete units or assumptions | Explains input, output, units, selected method and one assumption |
| Algorithm and code trace | Cannot identify update/computation | Traces a line with prompting | Connects pseudocode to one relevant line and predicts its output or a parameter effect |
| Validation | Relies on appearance or AI assurance | Names a check without evidence or limit | Applies the required and chosen checks and explains what each supports |
| Interpretation and reproducibility | Unsupported conclusion or missing dependencies | Partial physical explanation or run record | Explains principal result, limitation, fresh-session handoff and one material AI decision |

## Rehearsal Prompts

- What physical quantity does your main output represent, and what are its units?
- Which supplied method computes it, and why does that method match the question?
- Trace this update or calculation using the values in your own saved evidence.
- If one named parameter changes, what trend do you predict before rerunning?
- Show the required and chosen checks. What defect could each detect, and what remains untested?
- Which AI suggestion did you accept, change or reject? What independent evidence informed that decision?
- What must another student receive to reproduce this output from a fresh MATLAB session?

## Classroom Handoff

Use the existing Week 12 formative coursework if available. Request the edited capstone Live Script and necessary input files, principal output, concise evidence/checklist and remaining issue. Do not require a long new report or complete AI transcript. Do not invent a deadline. Week 13 retains the final investigation, individual defence and handoff.
