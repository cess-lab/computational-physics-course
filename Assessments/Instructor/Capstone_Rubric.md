# Capstone Rubric

**Course weight:** 15%  
**Group:** two or three students  
**Condition:** AI-assisted, bounded investigation, individual defence

Mark the final evidence against the approved Week 06–07 scope. The milestones are recorded separately in the course weight; the final rubric ensures that the completed packet carries its reasoning with its output.

## Criterion rubric

| Criterion | Weight within capstone | Secure Core evidence | Higher/distinction evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Model, assumptions, variables, and units | 15% | Correct approved model, named inputs/outputs, assumptions, and units are connected to the code/output. | Identifies a subtle assumption or explains a defensible bounded extension without losing clarity. |
| Pseudocode and supplied-method explanation | 15% | 5–8 ordered steps from inputs to principal output; method is explained in plain language. | Adapts the algorithm independently or compares methods with evidence. |
| Code tracing and justified modification | 20% | Traces a relevant starter section, changes one approved parameter/assumption, holds other comparison inputs fixed, and explains the effect. | Diagnoses a subtler issue or justifies an additional extension after the Core route. |
| Principal output and reproducibility | 15% | One labelled graph/table answers the question; clean-session steps, parameters, release/settings, and output are recorded. | Provides especially clear reproducibility or a carefully bounded secondary output. |
| Required and chosen validation evidence | 15% | Completes the approved required check and one different chosen check, states outcomes, and explains what each check tests. | Adds a well-justified independent check or method comparison. |
| Physical interpretation and limitation | 15% | Explains the main trend/result in physical language and states one relevant limitation without false precision. | Distinguishes numerical, parameter/data, and model limitations with evidence. |
| Individual defence and AI decision record | 5% | Each member traces a relevant section, answers one follow-up, and the group records material AI decisions and independent checks. | Handles a parameter/defect variant or follow-up transfer question precisely. |

## Performance guidance

- **Ready/strong:** all Core evidence is present, both checks are independent enough to be meaningful, the output is reproducible, and every member can explain the work.
- **Core completion:** the approved question is answered with correct model/units, one justified modification, one principal output, required plus chosen validation, interpretation, limitation, and member defence evidence, even if the code is not polished.
- **Revision required:** the group changes several inputs at once without control, omits units or the approved model, supplies a graph without checks, or cannot reproduce/defend the result.
- **Not evidenced:** no defensible model/output or no individual explanation. A complete AI-generated script by itself cannot receive full capstone credit.

## Milestone record

| Milestone | Week | Course weight | Marking evidence |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Problem-space selection and feasibility | 06 | 2% | narrow question, viable inputs, one modification, principal output, validation anchor |
| Approved model, method, and validation plan | 07 | 3% | model/units, method, 5–8 steps, required plus chosen check, expected trend/limitation |
| Development/process evidence | 08–11 | 3% | compact controlled comparison, output, validation, interpretation, AI decision evidence |
| Final bounded investigation | 13–14 | 5% | final packet and principal output |
| Individual defence and handoff | 13–14 | 2% | individual trace/follow-up and clean reproducibility handoff |

These entries total 15% of the course. Week 12 is a formative studio checkpoint, and Weeks 13–14 are the scheduled presentation, defence, and handoff sequence.
