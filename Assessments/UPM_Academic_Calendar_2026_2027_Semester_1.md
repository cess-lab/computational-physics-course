## PHY4605 placement

- **Test 1:** the broad UPM window is **Assessment I, academic Weeks 4–6**, with active blocks **26 October–08 November 2026** and **16–22 November 2026**. The PHY4605 target is **academic Week 6 (16–22 November 2026)**, after the Week 05 content and alongside Week 06, so the course sequence is not interrupted by the earlier block. Do not schedule it during the 09–15 November mid-semester break. Exact local date: **to be confirmed**.
- **Test 2:** the broad UPM window is **Assessment II, academic Weeks 8–12 (30 November 2026–03 January 2027)**. The PHY4605 target is **academic Week 12 (28 December 2026–03 January 2027)**, after the Week 11 content and alongside Week 12. Exact local date: **to be confirmed**.
- **Final examination:** the UPM final-examination window is **21 January–07 February 2027**, following the 18–20 January revision break. Exact PHY4605 examination date: **to be confirmed**.

The course assessment scopes remain unchanged: Test 1 covers Weeks 01–05, Test 2 covers Weeks 06–11, and the final examination is cumulative.
