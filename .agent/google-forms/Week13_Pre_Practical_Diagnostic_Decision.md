# Week 13 Pre-Practical Diagnostic Decision

## Decision

No separate Week 13 pre-practical diagnostic will be created for the current package.

## Evidence and rationale

- Week 13 is the capstone presentation, individual defence, and reproducibility handoff. It is not an active weekly practical introducing a new computational method.
- The student-facing Week 13 package is organised around an approved physical question, one controlled modification, a principal output, two validation checks, interpretation, limitation, defence evidence, and a fresh-session handoff.
- The local diagnostic coverage checklist records diagnostic planning only through Week 12, and the canonical live mapping currently contains only Weeks 01 and 02. No Week 13 question key, Form mapping, or Classroom diagnostic item is present.
- Therefore the weekly pre-practical diagnostic pattern does not apply to Week 13. This decision does not create, publish, attach, or modify any Google Form or Google Classroom coursework.

## Boundary

Google Classroom remains the official submission and feedback record for the Week 13 capstone evidence. If the lecturer later defines a separate Week 13 practical or requests a diagnostic for this package, revisit this decision before creating any diagnostic.
