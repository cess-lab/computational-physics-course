Why does it work?

For a function that is continuous on [a, b], opposite signs at the endpoints guarantee at least one root between them. Each update keeps this sign change and halves the interval.

If the midpoint is exactly a root, stop immediately. Otherwise, repeat until the chosen tolerance is met.

What should you check?

A small residual checks the target equation. It is different from an error bound on the root: the midpoint of a bracket has an error no greater than half that bracket’s width.
