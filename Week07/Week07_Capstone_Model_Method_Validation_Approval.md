## Method approval test

Your method is ready for approval only if your group can answer **yes** to all of the following:

- Does the method produce the principal output needed to answer the stated physical question?
- Can every member explain the main numerical idea in plain language before showing MATLAB code?
- Is the method appropriate for the type of problem rather than chosen only because a function name is familiar?
- Can the method be implemented by adapting the supplied course scaffold or starter Live Script rather than requiring an open-ended blank-page build?
- Are the numerical settings that control accuracy or resolution identified clearly?

## Validation-plan test

Your validation plan is ready only if:

- the required and chosen checks test different aspects of credibility;
- at least one check is independent of simply rerunning the same code;
- units are preserved from model inputs through the final output;
- the expected sign, trend, bound, known case, analytic reference, refinement behaviour, conservation relation, residual, or reproducibility evidence is stated before final interpretation; and
- the group can explain what result would cause the investigation to be rejected or revised.

## Approval outcome

The lecturer records one of three outcomes:

- **Approved:** model, method, and validation plan are sufficiently bounded to proceed.
- **Approved with revision:** proceed only after making the listed small correction.
- **Revise and resubmit:** one or more elements are not yet defensible or sufficiently bounded.

Approval does not certify that the final answer will be correct. It confirms that the investigation has a credible computational plan and a validation route before development continues.

## Submission boundary

Keep this milestone concise. Do not submit a complete final report or a fully polished final project. Week 08-11 will collect compact development/process evidence using the approved model, method, and validation plan. Google Classroom remains the official submission record.
