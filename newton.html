Why use a tangent?

Near a smooth point, the tangent approximates the curve. Its zero crossing gives the update xₙ₊₁ = xₙ − f(xₙ) / f′(xₙ). Evaluate the original curve again at that new estimate.

The tangent’s zero is generally not the curve’s zero. Unlike bisection, Newton’s method does not preserve a bracket or guarantee convergence. A poor starting estimate or a zero or very small derivative can make it fail.

What should you check?

A small residual checks the target equation; it is not itself an error bound on the estimated root. For the projectile model, the slope must be in metres per degree because the angle is in degrees.
