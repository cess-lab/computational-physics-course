# Week 04 Variant Bank

Use only after group submissions are locked. Safe variants include changing one resistor from 10 ohm to 12 ohm, changing one launch speed by 10--20%, asking for a different common-time comparison, changing one pendulum gravity value to another supplied planetary value, swapping the baseline case, inserting one `.*` versus `./` defect, or deliberately changing a second parameter and asking the student to identify why the comparison is no longer controlled.

Keep the defence focused on one-parameter control, array/loop tracing, labelled graph interpretation, and one known/reference check. Heatmaps and quantitative sensitivity measures are not Core defence requirements.
