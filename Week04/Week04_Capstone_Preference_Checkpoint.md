# Week 04 Capstone Preference Checkpoint

This is a **formative scope-familiarisation checkpoint**, not the Week 06 feasibility proposal. Review the assigned brief and its paired family in the current Capstone Briefing deck. You are not expected to have learned every numerical method yet, and this activity does not create a second project choice.

## Assigned brief families

| Brief family | Assigned investigations | Scope question to discuss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01–02 Projectile motion | Crosswind delivery with quadratic drag; elevated-terrain launch under wind shear | Which environmental or geometric effect is material to the assigned objective? |
| 03–04 Circuit behaviour | RC charging with leakage and temperature-dependent components; RC sensor circuit with internal source resistance and time-varying load | Which non-ideal component or loading effect is supportable? |
| 05–06 Radiation and detection | Decay with background/dead time; two-isotope mixture with efficiency/counting noise | What can the detector information distinguish? |
| 07–08 Thermal behaviour | Cooling with radiation/changing ambient; cooling with internal heat generation | Which energy-transfer effect changes the conclusion? |
| 09–10 Monte Carlo physics | Bounded 2D Ising ferromagnet; field of an irregular charged body | What does one random trial mean physically, and what evidence supports the estimate? |
| 11–12 Oscillatory motion | Damped mass–spring response; finite-angle pendulum motion | Which realism feature changes the response while preserving a defensible baseline? |
