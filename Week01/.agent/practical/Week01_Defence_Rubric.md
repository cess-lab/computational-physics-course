# Week 01 Individual Defence Rubric

Score each category from 0 to 3. The four-category mean is the student's Week 01 entry in the cumulative 3% individual defence ledger.

| Category | 3 — secure | 2 — developing | 1 — limited | 0 — not evidenced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model and units | Correctly explains the supplied model, variables, units, and prediction. | Explains the main model with a minor omission. | Names facts without a coherent model or unit link. | Cannot explain the model. |
| Code and output trace | Traces the relevant line and links it accurately to the output. | Traces most of the line/output link. | Recognises fragments without explaining the computation. | Cannot trace the relevant code or output. |
| Validation or perturbation | Applies the check or variant correctly and explains why it is useful. | Applies it with a minor prompt. | States a check without connecting it to the result. | No valid check or response. |
| Physical interpretation and AI judgement | Gives a defensible physical conclusion and identifies how AI output was checked. | Gives a plausible conclusion with limited AI judgement. | Gives a vague conclusion or disclosure. | No physical interpretation or judgement. |

Average the first completed defence entry for each member before any member receives a second entry; this establishes an equal Week 01 individual-evidence baseline. After every member has one completed entry, average all completed entries for the student's cumulative 3% component. An absence receives no score until the recorded make-up defence is completed.
