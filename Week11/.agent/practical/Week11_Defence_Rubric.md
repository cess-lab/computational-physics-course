# Week 11 Individual Defence Rubric

Score the selected student out of 8. Record every attempt as a new row in `Week11_Defence_Rotation_Ledger.csv`; use `Week11_Defence_Member_Summary.csv` only to identify who remains eligible for a first selection. Record one completed defence for every group member before selecting any member a second time. An absence remains pending until its recorded make-up defence is completed.

| Criterion | 0 marks | 1 mark | 2 marks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physical model, uncertain input, and units | Cannot identify the model or uncertain input. | Identifies part of the model or some units. | Clearly states the supplied model, baseline/lower/upper input, output, and relevant units. |
| One-at-a-time code/output trace | Cannot trace how a parameter case becomes an output. | Identifies one code line or case. | Traces baseline/lower/upper input -> same model -> output values -> reported range while stating what is held fixed. |
| Validation and assigned variant | No usable check. | States a check without applying it. | Applies a baseline, unit, trend, limiting/reference, or range check appropriately to the assigned variant. |
| Interpretation, limitation, and AI judgement | No supported physical conclusion. | Gives a conclusion with incomplete limitation reasoning. | Distinguishes parameter uncertainty from numerical error and model limitation, states a practical conclusion without false precision, and explains the recorded independent AI checks. |

The 3% cumulative individual-defence component averages each student's completed recorded entries. A long decimal result, smooth plot, or AI-generated explanation does not replace a justified uncertainty range, independent check, and physical interpretation.
