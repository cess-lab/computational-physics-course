# Week 05 Variant Bank

Use only after group submissions are locked. Safe variants include changing the projectile target range by 5--10%, changing one bracket endpoint and asking whether the sign-change condition still holds, changing the Newton starting angle within the low-angle region, changing the lens object distance, changing the RC threshold voltage, inserting a residual with reversed sign and asking whether the root changes, or inserting one degree/radian derivative defect.

Keep the defence focused on residual meaning, sign-changing brackets, midpoint or Newton update tracing, the single supplied stopping rule, substitution validation, and the physical unit of the root. Do not require uniqueness proof, multiple stopping-rule comparison, `fzero`, safeguarded Newton, or extreme small-derivative stress cases as Core defence evidence.
