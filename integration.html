Area becomes impulse

The area under the force–time curve is impulse. The trapezoidal rule uses a straight line between two samples. Simpson’s rule uses a quadratic through three equally spaced samples. Adding the panels estimates the total impulse.

Force is measured in N and time in s, so impulse is in N s, equivalent to kg m/s.

Check the result

The analytic reference is J = 12(0.8)[1 − exp(−2/0.8)] N s. The positive impulse must be smaller than the 24 N s rectangle formed by the initial force and the full duration.

Try 4, 8 and 16 intervals. Both methods use the same sampled force values. For this smooth example, both approach the reference as the intervals become narrower.
