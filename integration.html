Area becomes impulse

The area under the force–time curve is impulse. Each trapezoid estimates the area between two adjacent force samples; adding the strips estimates the total impulse.

Force is measured in N and time in s, so impulse is in N s, equivalent to kg m/s.

Check the result

The analytic reference is J = 12(0.8)[1 − exp(−2/0.8)] N s. The positive impulse must be smaller than the 24 N s rectangle formed by the initial force and the full duration.

Try 4, 8 and 16 intervals. For this smooth model, the trapezoidal estimate approaches the reference as the intervals become narrower.
