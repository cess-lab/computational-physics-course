# Week 10 Illustration Decision

No separate ImageGen illustration is justified for the Week 10 learning note or Live Script. The main novice representation barrier is seeing the relationship among a random step sequence, one trajectory, the distribution of repeated final positions, and sample-size stability. Native MATLAB plots expose those numerical relationships exactly and preserve the random-sampling evidence. The retained trajectory, histogram, and sample-size plots therefore serve as the appropriate scientific visuals.

For any later lecture-slide production, these MATLAB plots remain strict numerical/reference inputs only and must be redrawn as part of the complete slide image under the Codex-PPT course contract.
