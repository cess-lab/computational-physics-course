# Week 10 Individual Defence Rubric

Score the selected student out of 8. Record every attempt as a new row in `Week10_Defence_Rotation_Ledger.csv`; use `Week10_Defence_Member_Summary.csv` only to identify who remains eligible for a first selection. Record one completed defence for every group member before selecting any member a second time. An absence remains pending until its recorded make-up defence is completed.

| Criterion | 0 marks | 1 mark | 2 marks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physical trial, variables, and units | Cannot define one trial or connect variables to the context. | Defines part of the trial or some units. | Clearly defines one trial, repeated output, physical parameter, and relevant units/dimensionless quantities. |
| Random-sampling trace | Cannot identify how the random scaffold creates outcomes. | Identifies one random command or loop step. | Traces random draw -> event/step -> repeated outcome -> summary statistic in the assigned context. |
| Validation and assigned variant | No usable check. | States a check without applying it. | Applies a reference, symmetry, probability-range, step-rule, seed, or sample-size check appropriately to the variant. |
| Interpretation and AI judgement | No physical conclusion. | Gives an unsupported conclusion. | Distinguishes physical variability from estimate uncertainty, states a limitation, and explains the recorded independent AI checks. |

The 3% cumulative individual-defence component averages each student's completed recorded entries. Reproducing a seeded number or showing a smooth histogram does not replace explanation of the physical trial, sample-size evidence, validation, and physical interpretation.
